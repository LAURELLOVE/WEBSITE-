Medication Pathway

From prescription to treatment support

Pharmacy is integrated into the patient's treatment pathway and works in coordination with the prescribing clinical team.

1. PrescriptionThe treating physician selects the medication regimen or supportive treatment.
2. Pharmacy ReviewPrescription and medication requirements are reviewed within the clinical workflow.
3. Medication PreparationRequired medicines are organized and prepared or dispensed according to the prescribed treatment pathway.
4. Clinical DeliveryMedication is supplied for administration or patient use in coordination with the responsible clinical team.
5. Ongoing SupportPharmacy continues to support medication needs, treatment changes and follow-up prescriptions.
Oncology Integration

More than medication dispensing

COC Pharmacy is part of the multidisciplinary cancer-care system. The service supports treatment protocols, medication access and continuity of care for patients receiving chemotherapy, targeted treatment, immunotherapy, hormonal therapy, supportive medications and inpatient treatment when prescribed.

The pharmacy team works particularly closely with Medical Oncology and Nursing while also supporting Radiation Oncology, Surgery, Hospitalization, Urgent Care and Palliative Care.

Core pharmacy responsibilities

  • Support for COC's 30+ oncology treatment protocols.
  • Medication procurement, stock management and availability.
  • Storage and cold-chain management where required.
  • Prescription and medication verification within the clinical workflow.
  • Dispensing of oncology and supportive-care medications.
  • Coordination with physicians and nurses when treatment plans change.
  • Medication access for both outpatient and hospitalized patients.
