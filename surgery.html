SURGICAL CARE

Integrated cancer surgery at COC

Patients are evaluated according to diagnosis, stage, imaging, pathology, fitness for surgery and the role of other cancer treatments.

1

Evaluation

Clinical assessment with review of pathology, imaging and the patient's overall condition.

2

Multidisciplinary Planning

Surgery is coordinated with medical oncology, radiation oncology and other specialties when combined treatment is indicated.

3

Operation & Hospital Care

Operative care is supported by nursing, laboratory, imaging, pharmacy and inpatient services.

4

Pathology & Follow-up

Surgical pathology and postoperative review guide recovery and decisions about additional cancer treatment.

SURGICAL SERVICES

Specialist surgical care

COC brings surgical specialties together around the needs of each cancer patient.

General Oncology Surgery

General surgical evaluation and operative management of appropriate cancer cases.

Breast Surgery

Breast-conserving surgery and mastectomy, with axillary and sentinel-node procedures when clinically indicated.

Gynecologic Surgery

Surgical care for appropriate gynecologic conditions and cancers integrated with the oncology team.

Urologic Surgery

Specialist urologic evaluation and surgery, including care of patients with urologic malignancies.

ENT / Head & Neck

Ear, Nose & Throat and head-and-neck surgical assessment coordinated with radiotherapy and systemic treatment when required.

Perioperative Coordination

Preoperative assessment, hospitalization, postoperative care and coordination of pathology and subsequent oncology treatment.
