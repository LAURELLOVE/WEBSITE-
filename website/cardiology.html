Cardio-Oncology Pathway

Protecting the heart throughout cancer treatment

When a cancer patient needs cardiovascular review, the cardiology and oncology teams can coordinate care without fragmenting the treatment pathway.

1. Baseline AssessmentReview cardiac history, symptoms, medications and risk factors.
2. Cardiac TestingECG, echocardiography and other clinically indicated assessments.
3. Risk ReviewEvaluate cardiac status in relation to the proposed cancer treatment.
4. Treatment MonitoringFollow cardiovascular status during chemotherapy, targeted therapy, immunotherapy or radiotherapy when needed.
5. Long-Term Follow-upContinue cardiac care after treatment when ongoing monitoring is indicated.
Integrated Multidisciplinary Care

Oncology and cardiology working together

Some anticancer therapies may affect cardiovascular function or may require additional monitoring in patients with existing heart disease. COC's integrated model allows the oncology team and cardiology team to coordinate evaluation, treatment decisions and follow-up within the same center.

Connected services: Medical Oncology, Radiation Oncology, Diagnostic Imaging, Pharmacy, Laboratory Services, Nutrition, Hospitalization and Palliative Care.

Patients may be referred for

  • Chest pain, shortness of breath, palpitations or edema.
  • Hypertension or known cardiovascular disease.
  • Baseline cardiac assessment before selected cancer treatments.
  • Monitoring during therapies with potential cardiovascular effects.
  • Evaluation of new cardiac symptoms during treatment.
  • General cardiology care unrelated to cancer.
