Diagnostic Excellence

Histopathology & Tissue Diagnosis

Accurate tissue diagnosis is the foundation of cancer care. COC coordinates biopsy processing, microscopic assessment and specialist pathology support to guide treatment decisions.

Biopsy ProcessingSurgical PathologyCytologySpecialized Testing
What We Do

Pathology integrated with the oncology pathway

Histopathology findings are coordinated with imaging, clinical examination and multidisciplinary discussion so the oncology team can establish diagnosis and plan care.

1

Biopsy & Specimen Processing

Receiving, preparation and processing of tissue specimens from biopsy and surgery.

2

Histologic Diagnosis

Microscopic evaluation supports confirmation of malignancy, tumor type and relevant diagnostic features.

3

Cytology

Cytologic assessment is used where appropriate as part of the diagnostic pathway.

4

Specialized / Referral Testing

Selected immunohistochemistry and other specialized studies can be coordinated with external collaborators when required.

Patient Pathway

From biopsy to treatment planning

1. Specimen CollectionBiopsy or surgical specimen obtained by the treating clinical team.
2. Laboratory ProcessingFixation, preparation, embedding, sectioning and staining.
3. Pathology ReviewMicroscopic examination and diagnostic interpretation.
4. Additional StudiesSpecial stains or immunohistochemistry where clinically indicated.
5. Oncology PlanningResults are integrated into multidisciplinary treatment decisions.
