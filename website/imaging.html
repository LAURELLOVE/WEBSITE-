Our Imaging Services

Diagnostic imaging integrated with oncology care

Imaging is central to modern cancer care. COC's Diagnostic Imaging service works with Radiation Oncology, Medical Oncology, Surgery and other clinical teams to provide anatomical information needed throughout the patient's care pathway.

CT Imaging

Cross-sectional imaging to support diagnosis, staging, treatment assessment and follow-up when clinically indicated.

MRI

Magnetic resonance imaging provides detailed soft-tissue assessment for selected clinical and oncology indications.

Ultrasound

Accessible real-time imaging for selected abdominal, pelvic, breast, soft-tissue and other clinical evaluations.

Oncology Applications

Supporting decisions before, during and after treatment

Diagnosis & Characterization

Imaging helps identify and characterize abnormalities that require further clinical evaluation.

Staging

CT, MRI and ultrasound can help define disease extent and inform multidisciplinary treatment decisions.

Treatment Support

Diagnostic imaging contributes to surgical planning and complements the dedicated CT simulation workflow used by Radiation Oncology.

Response & Follow-up

Comparison imaging can help clinicians assess treatment response and monitor patients after therapy.

Imaging Technology

Available modalities at COC

COC's imaging platform includes CT, a Hitachi 0.35T MRI system and ultrasound. Nuclear Medicine and SPECT-CT are maintained as a distinct clinical service with their own dedicated page.

Clinical CT

Diagnostic CT imaging for appropriate clinical indications and oncology evaluation.

Hitachi 0.35T MRI

MRI capability for selected neurological, musculoskeletal, abdominal, pelvic and oncology applications.

Ultrasound

Non-ionizing real-time imaging that supports a broad range of diagnostic evaluations.

Separate from Radiation Oncology CT Simulation: COC's Philips Big Bore CT simulator is part of the Radiation Oncology treatment-planning workflow. It should not be confused with routine diagnostic imaging.
