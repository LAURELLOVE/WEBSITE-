COC Affiliate Physician & Hospital Network

One Network. One Pathway. Better Cancer Care Closer to Home.

COC's hub-and-spoke model connects physicians, clinics and hospitals to the specialized oncology expertise, diagnostics and treatment capabilities of Cameroon Oncology Center while keeping local healthcare teams actively involved in patient care.

The Network Model

COC as the specialized oncology hub

Affiliate physicians and hospitals provide local access, referral, selected supportive services and follow-up according to their capabilities, while COC provides specialized multidisciplinary cancer care.

Core principle: referral to COC should strengthen—not replace—the relationship between patients and their local physicians or hospitals. The objective is continuous communication, coordinated treatment and appropriate shared follow-up.
Partnership Journey

From mapping to continuous quality improvement

A structured nine-step pathway supports safe activation, clear responsibilities and long-term partnership growth.

1

Map Institution

Identify physicians, clinics and hospitals in target regions and collect basic service information.

Lead: Marketing & Network Development
2

Meet Leadership

Meet institutional leadership and identify clinical focal persons and collaboration opportunities.

Lead: Relationship Manager
3

Assess Service Gaps

Review current capabilities, referral challenges, training needs and potential collaboration areas.

Lead: Clinical & Quality Team

Lead: Network Development Committee

Lead: Operations & Referral Coordination

Lead: Partnership Coordinator
9

Review Quarterly

Review clinical outcomes, referral quality, patient satisfaction and partnership performance.

Lead: Quality & Network Review Committee
Four Affiliate Levels

A level for every type of partner

The network is designed for individual physicians, clinics and hospitals with different levels of oncology capability.

1

Referral Partner

Provides initial assessment and refers patients to COC for diagnostic confirmation, specialist consultation and treatment.

2

Service Partner

Provides selected basic oncology-support or diagnostic services and refers patients to COC when advanced care is required.

3

Collaborative Care Partner

Provides broader shared-care services, follow-up and co-management according to local capacity.

4

Advanced Collaborative Partner

Works with COC in a highly integrated model for comprehensive oncology services, joint programs and training.

Patient Pathway

One patient. One connected care pathway.

The network is designed to reduce fragmentation while expanding access to specialized cancer care.

1. Local EvaluationPatient is assessed by a community physician or partner hospital.
2. Referral to COCRecords and referral information are transferred for specialist assessment.
3. Specialized CareCOC provides required oncology diagnostics, planning and treatment.
4. Communication BackThe referring team receives treatment summaries and next-step recommendations.
5. Shared Follow-upOngoing care is coordinated between COC and the local healthcare team when appropriate.
Why Join

Benefits across the network

Partnership should create measurable value for patients, clinicians and participating institutions.

For Physicians & Hospitals

  • Access to COC's multidisciplinary oncology expertise.
  • Clear referral and escalation pathways.
  • Continued involvement in the patient's care.
  • Shared follow-up opportunities.
  • Professional education and capacity-building opportunities.

For Patients & Families

  • Faster access to appropriate specialized care.
  • Better coordination between local and specialist teams.
  • Reduced delays in diagnosis and treatment.
  • More care delivered closer to home when clinically appropriate.
  • Continuity across diagnosis, treatment and follow-up.

For the Network

  • Improved referral completeness.
  • Stronger communication and accountability.
  • Shared quality improvement.
  • Better use of specialist resources.
  • Long-term institutional relationships and network growth.
