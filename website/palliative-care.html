Palliative & Supportive Care

Relieving symptoms, preserving dignity and improving quality of life throughout the cancer journey.

Support alongside cancer treatment

Palliative care can be introduced at any stage of serious illness and can be provided alongside active cancer treatment. At COC, supportive care focuses on symptom relief, communication, psychosocial support, nutrition and coordination with the oncology team.

Pain & Symptom Management

Assessment and management of pain and other distressing cancer- or treatment-related symptoms.

Accelerated Palliative Radiotherapy

For selected patients, short radiotherapy schedules may provide pain relief or rapid tumor control, with treatment often completed within one week.
