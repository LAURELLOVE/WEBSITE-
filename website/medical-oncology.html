PATIENT PATHWAY

From diagnosis to treatment and follow-up

Medical oncology care is individualized according to cancer type, stage, pathology, biomarker findings, previous treatment, performance status and patient goals.

1

Consultation & Staging

Review of pathology, imaging, clinical status, comorbidities and prior treatment to define the clinical problem.

2

Treatment Planning

An evidence-based systemic treatment plan is selected and coordinated with surgery, radiation oncology and supportive services when indicated.

3

Treatment Delivery

Therapy is administered in the infusion center or private rooms with nursing observation, pharmacy support and monitoring for treatment-related effects.

4

Response & Follow-up

Clinical review, laboratory testing and imaging are used to assess response, manage toxicity and adjust treatment when necessary.

TREATMENT OPTIONS

Systemic therapies available at COC

Treatment selection depends on diagnosis and clinical indication. Not every therapy is appropriate for every patient.

💧

Conventional Chemotherapy

Protocol-based cytotoxic treatment delivered with clinical, nursing and pharmacy oversight.

🎯

Targeted Therapy

Biologically directed medicines used when tumor characteristics and treatment indications support their use.

🛡️

Immunotherapy

Immune-based systemic treatment for selected cancers according to clinical indication and access.

⚕️

Hormonal Therapy

Endocrine treatment for hormone-sensitive cancers such as selected breast and prostate cancers.

🔗

Combined-Modality Care

Systemic therapy coordinated with radiotherapy or surgery when combined treatment is clinically appropriate.

❤️

Supportive & Palliative Treatment

Symptom control, treatment of complications and systemic therapy aimed at extending survival or improving quality of life where appropriate.

PATIENT-CENTERED DELIVERY

Treatment in the infusion center or private rooms

COC delivers medical oncology treatment in a dedicated infusion setting and, where clinically appropriate, in private patient rooms. This allows treatment to be coordinated around comfort, monitoring requirements and the patient's broader clinical needs.

✓Dedicated oncology nursing support
✓On-site pharmacy coordination
✓Laboratory monitoring
✓Access to urgent medical review
✓Nutrition and psychosocial support
✓Multidisciplinary coordination
