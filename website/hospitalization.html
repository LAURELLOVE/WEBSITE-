Inpatient Care at COC

Designed around patient condition and clinical need

Most hospitalized patients at COC are receiving cancer-related care. Patient acuity is classified on admission and can be changed at any time by the physician generalist or oncologist as the patient's condition changes.

Onsite Medical Presence

A physician generalist is physically present onsite 24 hours a day, with oncologists available onsite for emergency escalation.

Protected Nursing Ratio

A nurse is assigned to care for no more than six hospitalized patients, supporting closer observation and more responsive care.

Private-Room Focus

Approximately 90% of hospitalized patients are accommodated in private rooms, with a small number of four-patient rooms also available.

Daily Oncology Review

Hospitalized oncology patients are reviewed twice daily by an oncologist, with additional physician generalist reviews based on acuity.

Four-Level Acuity System

Care intensity increases with patient need

COC uses a four-level inpatient classification system so nursing and physician support can be matched to the patient's condition and adjusted promptly when necessary.

Level 1

Standard Accommodation

Stable patients using hospitalization primarily for accommodation, observation, treatment support or routine inpatient care.

Level 2

Needs Stabilization

Patients who are clinically sicker and require stabilization or closer monitoring before treatment, surgery or another planned intervention.

Level 3

High-Observation Care

Patients requiring substantially closer medical observation, including physician review approximately every 2–3 hours when clinically indicated.

Level 4

Intensive Care

Patients requiring intensive-care-level monitoring and support. Escalation to this level is determined by the treating physician or oncologist.

Continuous Clinical Oversight

Oncologist review plus physician generalist coverage

Hospitalized oncology patients are reviewed by an oncologist twice each day. Between oncology rounds, the onsite physician generalist provides continuing medical oversight and reviews patients at intervals determined by acuity and clinical condition.

Because patient condition can change rapidly during cancer treatment, the assigned level is not fixed. The physician generalist or oncologist can increase or decrease the level when appropriate.

Inpatient support includes

  • 24-hour onsite physician generalist coverage.
  • Emergency escalation to onsite oncology support.
  • Nursing care matched to patient acuity.
  • Medication and pharmacy support.
  • Pain and symptom management.
  • Coordination with Radiation Oncology, Medical Oncology, Surgery, Imaging and Laboratory services.
  • Private-room accommodation for most patients.
  • Onsite restaurant access for patients, carers and visitors.
