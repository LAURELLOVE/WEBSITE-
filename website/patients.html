Before Your First Visit

What to bring with you

Bringing prior medical information helps the COC team understand what has already been done and may reduce unnecessary delays or repeat testing.

1

Medical Records

Bring previous consultation notes, discharge summaries, operative reports and referral letters if available.

2

Imaging & Pathology

Bring CT, MRI, ultrasound, mammography, pathology reports, biopsy results and image CDs where available.

3

Medication List

Bring a current list of medicines, allergies and relevant chronic medical conditions.

4

Insurance & Identification

Bring identification, insurance information and contact details for a trusted person involved in your care.

Your COC Patient Journey

One coordinated pathway

Your care may involve several departments, but the pathway is designed to remain connected from entry through treatment and follow-up.

1

Registration

Patient information, records and initial needs are documented.

2

Nurse Navigation

A navigator helps organize the next step in the oncology pathway.

3

Specialist Review

Radiation, medical, surgical or other specialist assessment as needed.

4

Diagnostics & Staging

Imaging, pathology and laboratory investigations are coordinated.

5

Multidisciplinary Plan

The treatment approach is developed around diagnosis, stage and patient needs.

6

Treatment

Radiotherapy, systemic therapy, surgery or supportive care as indicated.

7

Follow-up

Navigation, surveillance, supportive care and survivorship continue after treatment.

Universal Closed-Loop Navigation

Your care stays connected.

COC's navigation model is designed to reduce confusion and lost follow-up. The navigator helps connect the patient to the appropriate specialist, confirms the next step after consultation, supports scheduling and helps coordinate longitudinal follow-up.

Hospitalization & Caregivers

What families should know

Inpatient care at COC is organized around patient acuity, privacy, safety and clear caregiver responsibilities.

Private-Room-Centered Care

Approximately 90% of hospitalized patients stay in private rooms. A small number of four-patient rooms are also available.

24-Hour Clinical Support

A physician generalist is physically onsite 24 hours, hospitalized oncology patients are seen twice daily by an oncologist, and nursing care is limited to a maximum of six patients per nurse.

Caregiver Limits

Private room: maximum 3 caregivers during the day and 2 at night. Non-private room: maximum 1 caregiver.

Bedding is changed daily between 8:00–10:00. Patients and caregivers are expected to protect hospital property, follow room safety rules and immediately notify staff about spills, biological waste or damage. Read inpatient information →
Support Beyond Treatment

Whole-person cancer care

Cancer care extends beyond chemotherapy, radiotherapy and surgery. COC provides supportive services across the treatment journey.

Psychology

Emotional, family and caregiver support through diagnosis, treatment and survivorship.

Nutrition

Nutrition screening, counseling and support for treatment-related nutrition problems.

Palliative Care

Pain, symptom and quality-of-life support alongside active cancer treatment when appropriate.

Pharmacy

Medication counseling, oncology treatment support and day/night pharmacy coverage.
