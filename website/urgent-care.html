More Than Cancer Care

Urgent medical support for patients and the community

Although COC is a comprehensive oncology center, its onsite medical team also evaluates patients who present with non-cancer emergencies and common medical conditions.

24-Hour Clinical Presence

Onsite physician support around the clock

COC maintains physician generalist presence onsite 24 hours a day. The general physician team provides first assessment, stabilization, treatment and escalation to oncology or other specialist services when needed.

For cancer patients, urgent care is closely linked with Medical Oncology, Radiation Oncology, Surgery, Pharmacy, Laboratory, Imaging, Hospitalization and Palliative Care.

Common reasons patients may present

  • Severe pain or sudden worsening of symptoms.
  • Fever, suspected infection or malaria.
  • Vomiting, diarrhea, dehydration or weakness.
  • Shortness of breath or chest symptoms.
  • Bleeding or treatment-related complications.
  • Accidents and minor trauma requiring assessment.
  • Routine medical review and general health checkups.
  • Pregnancy-related emergencies requiring immediate medical attention.
Emergency Obstetric Experience

Unexpected emergencies are stabilized when they arrive

Over the past seven years, COC has delivered more than 70 babies who arrived through emergency circumstances. This reflects the broader role of the onsite medical team in responding to urgent community needs when patients present for help.

Scope of service: COC remains primarily an oncology center. General urgent care supports immediate assessment and stabilization, with referral or transfer when a patient's needs require services beyond COC's available capability.
