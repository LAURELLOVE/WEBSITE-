Clinical Scope

Women's health integrated with comprehensive oncology

COC connects gynecology directly with histopathology, imaging, surgical oncology, radiation oncology, medical oncology and supportive care so women can move efficiently from diagnosis to treatment.

C

Cervical Cancer

Evaluation, staging and multidisciplinary treatment planning for cervical cancer, including coordinated surgery, radiotherapy and systemic therapy when indicated.

O

Ovarian Cancer

Assessment of adnexal and ovarian masses with coordinated imaging, pathology, surgery and medical oncology input.

E

Endometrial Cancer

Diagnosis and treatment planning for cancers of the uterine lining, coordinated with pathology, imaging and surgical services.

G

General Gynecology

Assessment of abnormal bleeding, pelvic symptoms and other gynecologic conditions that may require diagnostic or oncology referral.

Gynecologic Cancer Pathway

From first evaluation to individualized treatment

Patients can move through a coordinated pathway at COC, reducing fragmentation between diagnosis, staging, treatment and follow-up.

1. Gynecologic AssessmentSymptoms, clinical examination and review of prior screening or test results.
2. Diagnostic TestingLaboratory testing, ultrasound and other investigations as clinically required.
3. ImagingCT, MRI and ultrasound for diagnosis, staging and treatment planning.
4. Tissue DiagnosisBiopsy, cytology or surgical pathology when required to confirm diagnosis.
5. Multidisciplinary PlanningGynecology, surgery, radiation oncology and medical oncology coordinate the treatment plan.
6. Treatment & Follow-upSurgery, radiotherapy, chemotherapy or combined treatment according to diagnosis and stage.
Cervical Cancer Care

A coordinated program for one of the region's major women's cancers

COC provides a direct pathway from gynecologic assessment and tissue diagnosis to staging, treatment planning, radiotherapy, medical oncology and follow-up. This multidisciplinary model is especially important for women who present with locally advanced disease and require combined-modality treatment.

Connected services: Gynecology, Histopathology, Diagnostic Imaging, Radiation Oncology, Medical Oncology, Surgery, Pharmacy, Nutrition, Psychology and Palliative Care.

When women may be referred to Gynecology

  • Abnormal vaginal bleeding or persistent discharge.
  • Pelvic pain, pressure or a pelvic mass.
  • Abnormal cervical screening or suspected cervical lesion.
  • Ovarian or uterine abnormality identified on imaging.
  • Need for gynecologic biopsy or surgical evaluation.
  • Follow-up during or after treatment for a gynecologic cancer.
