Clinical Scope

Urologic care integrated with oncology

Patients can be assessed by Urology and then linked directly to pathology, imaging, radiation oncology, medical oncology or surgery when specialized cancer treatment is required.

P

Prostate Cancer

Assessment, diagnosis, staging and multidisciplinary treatment planning for localized, locally advanced and metastatic prostate cancer.

B

Bladder Cancer

Evaluation of hematuria and bladder tumors with coordinated diagnostic work-up and referral for surgery, systemic therapy or radiotherapy as indicated.

K

Kidney & Testicular Cancer

Specialist evaluation and coordination of imaging, pathology, surgery and oncology treatment for renal and testicular malignancies.

U

General Urology

Assessment of urinary obstruction, lower urinary tract symptoms and other urologic conditions that may occur alongside cancer care.

Prostate Cancer Pathway

From evaluation to individualized treatment

COC brings diagnostic and treatment services together so patients can move through the prostate-cancer pathway with fewer disconnected referrals.

1. Clinical AssessmentHistory, symptoms, examination and risk review.
2. PSA / Laboratory TestingPSA-based assessment and other tests when clinically indicated.
3. ImagingCT, MRI, ultrasound and other imaging as needed for diagnosis or staging.
4. Tissue DiagnosisBiopsy and histopathology when required to establish diagnosis.
5. Multidisciplinary PlanningUrology, radiation oncology and medical oncology coordinate the treatment strategy.
6. Treatment & Follow-upSurgery, radiotherapy, systemic therapy, hormonal therapy or surveillance according to the clinical plan.
Multidisciplinary Cancer Care

One center, coordinated decisions

Urologic oncology at COC is supported by the wider cancer team. Complex cases can be discussed across specialties so diagnosis, staging and treatment recommendations are coordinated rather than fragmented.

Core collaborating services: Diagnostic Imaging, Histopathology, Radiation Oncology, Medical Oncology, Surgical Oncology, Pharmacy, Nutrition and Palliative Care.

When patients may be referred to Urology

  • Elevated or concerning PSA results.
  • Difficulty urinating or urinary obstruction.
  • Blood in the urine.
  • Prostate, bladder, kidney or testicular mass or imaging abnormality.
  • Need for urologic surgical assessment.
  • Urologic symptoms arising during or after cancer treatment.
