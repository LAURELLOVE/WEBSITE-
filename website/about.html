Our Story

A long-term commitment to cancer care in Cameroon

Cameroon Oncology Center was conceived in response to the severe shortage of access to modern cancer treatment, particularly radiotherapy. The project progressed from land acquisition and construction to installation of its first linear accelerator and treatment of its first patient on April 1, 2019. Since then, COC has expanded into a comprehensive multidisciplinary center bringing diagnosis, treatment, supportive care, education and research together on one campus.

2010

The vision begins

The Cameroon Oncology Project is conceived to expand access to modern cancer care.

2012

Land acquired

A permanent site is secured at Bekoko-Dibombari on the outskirts of Douala.

2013

Construction begins

Development of the COC campus begins, creating the physical foundation for a dedicated oncology center.

2016

First major construction phase completed

The facility reaches a major milestone as clinical infrastructure is prepared for oncology services.

2018

First linear accelerator installed

Modern external-beam radiotherapy capability is established at COC.

2019

First patient treated — April 1

COC begins clinical treatment with a small multidisciplinary team, radiotherapy, chemotherapy and laboratory support.

Today

Comprehensive multidisciplinary care

COC now integrates Radiation Oncology, Medical Oncology, Surgery, Imaging, Nuclear Medicine, Histopathology, Laboratory Services, Cardiology, Psychology, Nutrition, Pharmacy, Hospitalization and other specialties.
