Community Impact

Screening is most valuable when it leads to the right next step.

COC provides the clinical expertise while the Cameroon Cancer Foundation leads community engagement, mobilization and outreach programming.

1

Awareness & Sensitization

Community education helps people recognize warning signs, understand screening and seek care earlier.

2

Cancer Screening

Programs focus particularly on breast, cervical and prostate cancer, with general health screening incorporated where appropriate.

3

Referral & Follow-up

People with suspicious findings are directed for further clinical assessment, imaging, pathology and specialist oncology care when needed.

Cameroon Cancer Foundation

A dedicated community partner for prevention and early detection

The Cameroon Cancer Foundation (CCF) is a separate collaborating organization that works closely with COC on cancer awareness, community mobilization and screening. CCF helps take services beyond the walls of the hospital while COC provides the technical and clinical expertise needed for screening, evaluation and referral.

From Screening to Care

A closed pathway from community contact to specialist evaluation

Screening is not the endpoint. Suspicious findings should lead to timely diagnostic confirmation and appropriate care.

1. Community EducationAwareness, risk information and invitation to screening.
2. ScreeningBreast, cervical, prostate and basic health screening as appropriate.
3. Suspicious FindingPeople who need further evaluation are identified.
4. Diagnostic ReferralClinical consultation, imaging, laboratory or pathology assessment.
5. Treatment / Follow-upCOC or another appropriate facility coordinates next steps.

Core screening priorities

  • Breast cancer: awareness, clinical evaluation and referral for imaging/pathology when indicated.
  • Cervical cancer: screening, prevention education and referral of abnormal findings.
  • Prostate cancer: education, clinical assessment and PSA testing where indicated.
  • General health: basic health checks may be added to community programs to improve participation and identify other health needs.
Prevention Matters

Earlier detection can change the patient journey

Community programs are designed to reduce delays in presentation, help identify suspicious findings earlier and connect people to appropriate diagnostic and treatment services.

COC and CCF also use outreach activities to collect data that can strengthen future prevention programs, education and research in Cameroon.
